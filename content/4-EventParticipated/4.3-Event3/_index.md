# Summary Report: “FCAJ Agentic AI Build Week”

### Overview of the Event

The **FCAJ Agentic AI Build Week** was an intensive, hands-on hackathon and engineering sprint focused on building real-world applications powered by **Agentic AI** technologies. Over the course of the week, participating teams designed, developed, and optimized autonomous AI agents using cutting-edge frameworks and deployed them on AWS cloud infrastructure.

The event combined technical learning, rapid prototyping, and business modeling, culminating in a pitching session where teams demonstrated their solutions to a panel of technology experts and business leaders.

---

### Key Challenges & Lessons Learned

Developing autonomous agentic systems in a limited time frame introduced several complex engineering and design challenges:

* **Agent Orchestration & Workflow Debugging**: Building agents that can plan, self-correct, and use tools recursively required careful design. We learned how to handle loop detection and guide the agent’s execution path safely.
* **Integrating Advanced AI Frameworks**: We gained hands-on experience integrating tools like **LangFuse** for prompt tracking and observability, **Apify** for web scraping, and custom agent wrappers like **TinyFish**.
* **Prompt Engineering & Cost Management**: Optimizing system prompts to ensure consistent agent output while managing LLM token consumption and costs was a critical optimization task.
* **Rapid MVP Generation**: Translating a complex conceptual idea into a working prototype under tight deadlines taught us the value of selecting the right AWS serverless and containerized services for quick deployment.

---

### Pitching Sessions & Business Modeling

The build week concluded with pitching presentations where each team had to explain their product value, technical architecture, and business viability:

* **Identifying Real-world Challenges**: In the pitching sessions, we presented the user pain points and the specific challenges our Agentic AI solution aims to solve (e.g. automating complex manual workflows).
* **Value Creation & Delivery Canvas**: We mapped out our system’s key partners (AWS, LangFuse, Apify, TinyFish), core resources, customer segments, channels, and value propositions. This exercise helped us think beyond coding and evaluate the product's actual business feasibility.

---

### Personal Experience

Participating in the **FCAJ Agentic AI Build Week** was a challenging yet rewarding experience. It pushed us to learn and implement advanced AI concepts rapidly while collaborating closely under pressure.

The opportunity to pitch our solution and receive direct feedback from senior engineers and business mentors was invaluable. It helped us refine not only our technical architecture but also our presentation and communication skills, highlighting the importance of bridging the gap between deep technical implementation and business value.
