### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
Throughout my participation in the First Cloud AI Journey program, I experienced a highly professional yet exceptionally friendly learning and working environment. Everyone was always willing to help whenever I encountered roadblocks, even when the issues extended beyond my assigned tasks. This open, comfortable atmosphere encouraged me to confidently voice my ideas and take proactive ownership of my learning process.

**2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team**  
The mentors kept close track of my learning progress and task execution. When I faced challenges, they provided guidance and conceptual hints rather than direct answers, allowing me to understand the root causes and strengthen my analytical problem-solving skills. The admin team was also extremely responsive, facilitating smooth access to learning resources, promptly answering administrative queries, and communicating important updates efficiently.

**3. Relevance to Academic Major**  
The practical assignments and projects matched perfectly with my academic background in Information Technology and my interest in Cloud Computing. Getting hands-on exposure to core AWS services, Git/GitHub collaborative workflows, Docker, and real-world deployment pipelines allowed me to translate academic theory into solid engineering skills that went far beyond typical university coursework.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**  
The program was a fantastic accelerator for both my technical knowledge and soft skills. Beyond learning AWS and modern cloud tools, I considerably improved my self-learning capability, time management, collaboration with peers, technical writing, and presentation skills. These competencies have prepared me well for my future career in tech.

**5. Culture and Teamwork**  
The collaborative culture was one of the most outstanding aspects of the program. Knowledge sharing was highly encouraged, and everyone supported each other. The relationship between mentors, admins, and interns felt collaborative and barrier-free, allowing for comfortable and transparent communication. The workshops and sharing sessions also provided a great avenue to absorb valuable industry insights.

**6. Policies and Support for Interns**  
The program offers robust support for students, featuring structured learning materials, topical workshops, a clear roadmap, and engaging hands-on projects. Furthermore, the flexible schedule enabled me to seamlessly balance my university commitments with the internship, making it highly accommodating for students.

---

### Additional Feedback & Suggestions

* **What I appreciated most:** Being immersed in a practical, hands-on learning environment with a well-structured roadmap. Designing projects and deploying actual applications on AWS under the guidance of mentors who advocate for independent research and critical thinking has been incredibly empowering.
* **On-site Office Registration Challenges:** Due to the large number of interns, it was often quite competitive and difficult to register for on-site working days at the office. I suggest optimizing the booking system or scheduling office days more efficiently to give more interns the opportunity to work in person and interact directly.
* **More Networking Opportunities:** I recommend organizing more informal networking events or interactive activities between mentors and interns to foster stronger bonds, exchange experiences, and build a more connected community.

---

### Additional Comments

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the entire First Cloud AI Journey team for organizing such a high-quality, impactful program for students. Through FCAJ, I have not only acquired valuable cloud knowledge but also built a professional mindset and a strong foundation for self-directed learning. I wish the program continued growth and success, hoping it will continue to empower more students on their cloud computing journeys!
